Main finding

Missing matching contexts dominate the prediction gaps. All 71 exact opponent-scenario matches occurred in 1901. In this run, no complete opponent scenario was generated from 1903 onward.

Coverage gaps and fallback audit
  • 46,371 unit decisions had no prediction: 37,510 had no matching position context even at minimum 1, and 8,861 had evidence below the minimum of 3.
  • “Position” still includes game year, season, nation, unit type, location/coast, neighboring occupants, and nearby supply-center ownership.
  • On the same 7,098 decisions with broader position evidence, history-selected distributions achieved 4,872 top-four hits; position distributions achieved 5,329. There were 642 gains and 185 losses, for a net gain of 457.
  • Lowering minimum observations from 3 to 1 increased individual prediction coverage from 26.81% to 40.80%, but the aggregate opponent-scenario hit count stayed at 71 with width 32.
Interpretation and limitations
  • These are exact submitted-order matching metrics—not win rates, tactical evaluations, or calibrated probabilities.
  • The accepted subset excludes games incompatible with the current replay importer; exclusions may bias the results.
  • Reserved test games were not used for model training or scoring.
  • Cross-year fallback has now been evaluated on the same split: individual prediction coverage increased from 26.81% to 47.33%, adding 7,591 top-four order hits and 39 national-plan matches. Complete opponent-scenario matches remained 71. This fallback remains experimental.
  • The next experiment additionally relaxes neighboring-unit occupancy matching, only after existing prediction methods abstain. Nearby center ownership remains part of its context. Results for that experiment have not yet been measured.
